Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Introduction

List Buddy keeps your private lists on your device, and only lists you choose to share are stored on our servers. This policy explains what information the List Buddy app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

List Buddy is operated by 3G1C Technology Group ("we," "us"). This policy applies to the List Buddy app for iPhone and any related support channels. By using List Buddy, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

We collect only what List Buddy needs to work. Where each kind of information lives:

We do not access your location, contacts, browsing history, or advertising identifiers. If you add a profile photo, the app uses only the one picture you choose and never sees the rest of your photo library. List Buddy contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

If you change a list from Private to Shared, its contents are uploaded to our servers so your friends can see them. If you change it back to Private, we delete the shared copy from our servers.

How we use information

We use your information only to run List Buddy and keep it safe:

  • Create and secure your account, and let you sign in and reset your password
  • Connect you with friends and show shared lists to the friends you choose
  • Keep shared lists in sync between you and your friends
  • Check shared content and usernames for objectionable material (see Content moderation)
  • Review reports, and block or remove accounts that break our rules
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Meet legal obligations

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

Content moderation

To keep List Buddy free of illegal, abusive, and vulgar content, we automatically check text that other people will see before it is shared. Private lists are never checked, because they never leave your device.

  • On your device: a built-in word filter checks list names, items, category names, and usernames as you type. This check happens entirely on your phone.
  • On our servers: usernames, profile photos, and the text of shared lists are sent to OpenAI's moderation service, which scores them for categories such as harassment, hate, sexual content, and violence. We send only the text being checked, never your email address or password. OpenAI processes this text under its API data policies.
  • People: reports from users are reviewed by us. We aim to act on reports within 24 hours, which may include removing content or blocking the account that posted it.

If content is blocked, you will see a message asking you to change it. Automated checks can make mistakes; if you think something was blocked in error, contact us.

Storage, security, and retention

Our servers are hosted by Amazon Web Services in the United States. Information is encrypted while traveling between your device and our servers, and passwords are stored by Amazon Cognito in encrypted form; we never see them. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit access to your information to what is needed to run the app.

We keep information only as long as it is needed:

  • Private lists stay on your device until you delete them or delete the app.
  • Shared lists are kept until the owner deletes them or makes them private again.
  • Account and friends information is kept until you delete your account. Profile photos are kept until you remove them in Settings > Profile or delete your account.
  • Reports and moderation records are kept for up to 12 months to handle repeat abuse, then deleted.
  • Support messages are kept for up to 12 months after your issue is resolved.

Your choices and rights

You control your information directly in the app:

  • Edit or delete lists at any time. Making a shared list private removes it from our servers and from your friends' view.
  • Unfriend or block anyone. Blocked users cannot send you friend requests or see your shared lists.
  • Report any shared list or user you believe breaks our rules.
  • Delete your account in Settings. This permanently deletes your account, profile photo, friends, and shared lists from our servers within 30 days. Private lists on your device are not affected until you delete the app.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to receive a copy of it. To make a request, contact us at the address below. We will respond within the time required by law and will not treat you differently for making a request.

Children's privacy

List Buddy accounts are not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top. If a change affects how we use information you have already given us, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.

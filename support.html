Common questions

Who can see my lists?
Only you. Every list is private unless you switch it to Shared, and then only the approved friends you choose can see it.

How do I share a list?
Open the list, tap Private at the top right, switch it to Shared, and choose which friends can see it.

How do I stop sharing a list?
Open the list's settings and switch it back to Private. It disappears from your friends' view and is removed from our servers.

How do I block or unfriend someone?
Open their profile from your friends list and tap Unfriend or Block. Blocked users can't send you requests or see your shared lists.

Why was my text blocked?
List Buddy checks shared content and usernames for objectionable language. If you think something was blocked by mistake, email us with the text and we'll take a look.

How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings in the app and tap Delete Account. Your account, friends, and shared lists are permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

Will I lose my private lists if I get a new phone?
Private lists are stored only on your device. Deleting the app or switching phones removes them, so share or copy anything important first.
